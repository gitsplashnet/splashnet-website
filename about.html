Splashnet Technology Limited is an ICT solutions company delivering innovative, reliable and secure technology services across the United Kingdom and Nigeria.

Who we are

One team for your network, security and software.

Splashnet was founded with a simple vision: make good technology accessible to organisations of every size. Too often, businesses, schools and estates end up with a patchwork of contractors: one for cabling, another for cameras, another for the website, and nobody who sees the whole picture.

We specialise in networking infrastructure, security systems (CCTV & intercoms), managed web applications and custom software development. Because we cover the full stack, we design solutions that work together and support them for the long term.

Our motto, Global. Quality. Service., sums up how we work: international reach, professional standards, and support that doesn't disappear after installation day.
