Privacy policy

How Splashnet Technology Limited collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Splashnet Technology Limited ("Splashnet", "we", "us") provides ICT services from offices at 15 Mason Road, Clacton-on-Sea, Essex CO16 7AN, United Kingdom and 26b Gbemisola Street, off Allen Avenue, Ikeja, Lagos, Nigeria. For privacy questions, email info@splashnetech.com.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us when you contact us: name, organisation, email, phone number and the content of your message.
  • Information needed to deliver services you've engaged us for, such as site details and system access.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends when you visit this website (for example, IP address and browser type) via our hosting provider's logs.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes.
  • To deliver, support and maintain services under our agreement with you.
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we only share it with trusted suppliers where necessary to deliver our services.

How we protect it

Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel using role-based permissions. We use encryption, strong authentication and regular security updates to protect the information we hold.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and client records for as long as required by law or our contract with you.

Your rights

Depending on where you are, including under UK GDPR and Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal data, and to object to processing. To exercise these rights, email info@splashnetech.com. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) or the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our font provider (Google Fonts) may receive your IP address when fonts are loaded.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
