FAQ

Common questions.

What are the objectives of your services?

To give you secure internet connectivity, cloud-based web hosting, remote access to business applications, VPN solutions for safe remote work, and firewall and cybersecurity integration, all managed by one team.

What does your cybersecurity service involve?

We protect your business from digital threats with a layered approach: firewalls, anti-malware tools and data encryption to safeguard your information at all times, plus access controls, monitoring and staff awareness.

Can you help with password policies and staff training?

Yes. We recommend training all staff on secure password practices to prevent breaches, and we help businesses implement password policies and security frameworks aligned with global standards, so you avoid costly penalties.

Do you work with individuals and homes, or only businesses?

Both. We serve businesses, schools, estates and industrial sites, as well as individual homeowners who want CCTV, intercoms, reliable Wi-Fi or secure remote access.

Do you offer free site surveys?

Yes. For networking, CCTV and intercom projects we're happy to visit, assess the site and give you a clear proposal before you commit to anything.

Which areas do you cover?

We operate from Clacton-on-Sea, Essex in the UK and Ikeja, Lagos in Nigeria. Remote services such as web hosting, software and remote support are available wherever you are.

Can you work with our existing equipment?

Usually, yes. We'll assess what you already have, keep what's still fit for purpose and recommend upgrades only where they make a real difference.

How is pricing structured?

Installations are quoted per project after a survey. Managed security, hosting and support are available on monthly plans, scaled to the size of your organisation. Get in touch and we'll recommend the right plan.

How can I try your services?

Contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp or the contact form. We'll arrange a consultation to understand your needs and show you how our cyber defence and infrastructure solutions would work for you.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Absolutely. Every installation can be backed by a maintenance plan covering updates, health checks and priority support.

Planning a network, security or software project?

Tell us what you need. We'll come back within one working day with next steps and, where it helps, a free site survey.
